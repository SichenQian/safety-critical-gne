Fig.~\ref{fig:safety-margins}(a)--(c) shows that the tightened flow
maintains nonnegative collision, obstacle, and matrix-tree certificate
margins, while the untightened flow exhibits negative values of all three.
Negative collision and obstacle margins indicate violations of the
corresponding distance constraints. In contrast,
Fig.~\ref{fig:safety-margins}(d) shows that the actual algebraic
connectivity remains above $\underline{\lambda}_{\chi}=0.2$ for both flows,
with sampled minima of $2.54141$ and $1.91715$, respectively.
Over the saved samples with a negative untightened matrix-tree certificate
($t=76.9$--$79.5\,\mathrm{s}$), the algebraic connectivity ranges from
$2.06824$ to $2.15284$.
Both communication graphs have ten edges and one connected component
at every saved time on $[0,1200]\,\mathrm{s}$.
Thus, the negative certificate values illustrate the conservatism of
the sufficient matrix-tree condition, rather than a loss of the prescribed
spectral margin or graph connectivity in this simulation.
